# STS 2025: 3D CBCT Pulp Segmentation

## Overview

The STS 2025 Challenge focused on 3D volumetric segmentation of dental pulp from CBCT images. Accurate pulp segmentation is essential for endodontic treatment planning and dental diagnosis.

STS 2025 挑战赛聚焦于 CBCT 图像中牙髓的三维体积分割。精准的牙髓分割对根管治疗规划和口腔诊断至关重要。

## Task

- **Goal**: 3D CBCT volumetric segmentation of dental pulp
- **Input**: CBCT volumes
- **Output**: Volumetric pulp segmentation masks

## Key Methods

- **U-Mamba2**: A Mamba-based U-Net architecture for efficient 3D volumetric segmentation
- **Selective Spatial Cascade**: Multi-stage refinement strategy for improved segmentation accuracy
